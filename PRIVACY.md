# Privacy

- Your progress is stored only in your browser (IndexedDB). We never receive it.
- Analytics are cookieless and aggregate (page views, a few anonymous events). No item ids, no personal data.
- When suggestions are enabled, we store only the text you send, the optional nickname you give, and a one-way
  hash of your IP address used to limit spam.
